
# Qiong Li (she/her)

Qiong is currently a Research Associate at the University of Manchester. She obtained her PhD from Peking University in 2020. Qiong is an observational astronomer studying galaxy formation and large-scale structure during the first billion years of cosmic history. Her research focuses on the formation and evolution of high-redshift galaxies, protoclusters, and the cosmic web using deep imaging and spectroscopy from the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) and other multi-wavelength facilities.

Her research interests include:
 - Galaxy formation at cosmic dawn
 - High-redshift protoclusters and cluster assembly
 - The cosmic web and galaxy environments
 - Lyman-alpha blobs (LABs) and extended nebulae
