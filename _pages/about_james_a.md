
# James Arcidiacono (he/him)

James completed his integrated MPhys at the University of Manchester in 2025, having joined the group first as a summer intern and then for his Master's project under the supervision of Nathan Adams and Christopher Conselice. His project used JWST/NIRCam and HST/ACS-WFC imaging from the COSMOS-Web and COSMOS-3D surveys to select a sample of ~120 galaxies at 8 < z < 14 and constrain the bright ends of the rest-frame UV luminosity functions across this redshift range. He has spent the past year continuing this work as a Research Assistant in the group, and is now moving to the Cosmic Dawn Center (DAWN) at the University of Copenhagen to begin his PhD with Charlotte Mason, working on the sources and topology of reionization.

His research interests include:
 - The bright end of the high-redshift UV luminosity function and the UV luminosity/star formation rate density
 - When and how reionization occurred, and the size distribution of ionized bubbles
 - The halo–galaxy connection and clustering of galaxies at z > 8
 - Bursty star formation and the breakdown of the SFR–halo accretion link in the early Universe
